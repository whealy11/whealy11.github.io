Will Shane Healy

Hi, I'm Will. I'm a 24 year old Machine Learning Engineer living in Manhattan. I previously studied AI and Math at Stanford. Before that, I was an aspiring professional freestyle snowboarder competing on the world cup circuit. I made this website to share my writing. I was inspired to do this by my dad, who comfortably into his retirement got bored one summer and decided to write one of the most comprehensive and intuitive pieces of option pricing literature I've ever read. He focuses on building the theory from first principles, emphasizing the intuition behind concepts rather than merely presenting equations and proving theorems. I hope to present concepts across a range of topics here in a similar way: one which deeply engrains intuitions and also requires little prerequisite knowledge to understand. Luckily, I've found that these two goals often go hand in hand. I hope you enjoy. Feel free to reach out at healy.ws1@gmail.com.
